Privacy Policy — Interval Trainer

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Interval Trainer is an Android app for building workouts and running interval timers. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Interval Trainer Android app (package com.intervaltrainer.workout) and its development and staging builds.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean pobox-void, the developer of Interval Trainer. “You” means the person using the app.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. Your workouts, exercises, sets, history and preferences are stored on your device.
  • We do not receive your workout content, titles, descriptions, tags, calendar text or payment details.
  • The app sends anonymous diagnostic and usage telemetry so we can find crashes, fix failures and understand which features are used. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Free users see banner ads and may choose rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device or advertising identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Personalised ads are requested only after consent through Google’s UMP consent tool.
  • Pro users see no ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play; we only receive your entitlement status.
  • Calendar access and backups happen only when you choose those features.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in its private app storage:

  • Workout content: workouts, exercises, exercise sets, rests, durations, emoji, ordering and tags.
  • History: completed and incomplete workout snapshots, including the workout structure and performed items at the time of completion.
  • Preferences: settings such as prepare countdown, “Always skip last rest”, onboarding state and app preferences.
  • Entitlement cache: whether Pro features are unlocked, so the app can work offline. This is a status flag, not a purchase token or payment record.

This data stays on your device unless you explicitly export or back it up. We do not operate a user account or a server that receives your workout library.

You can delete individual items in the app, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data, subject to any Android backup you have enabled on your device.

2. Anonymous app telemetry

The app collects anonymous telemetry in every build. It is always on because it is used to keep the app reliable and secure. It is designed not to identify you and is not linked to an account, advertising identifier or device identifier.

Telemetry may include:

  • A random session ID and flow ID used to correlate events in one app session or journey.
  • Screen names, journey types and journey steps.
  • App version, build type, environment, device model, OS version, network type and screen orientation.
  • Feature usage counts and duration buckets, such as the number of exercises in a workout or whether a workout lasted 5–15 minutes.
  • Crash and ANR (application not responding) diagnostics, such as exception type, a redacted message, and numeric runtime details like battery percentage, free storage and free heap.
  • Structured logs, error classes, API failure routes, HTTP methods, status codes and latency buckets.
  • Premium entitlement state, purchase-flow outcomes and backup/print operation outcomes, using public feature names and result categories only.
  • Ad consent status and whether an ad request was personalised. This does not include ad unit IDs, advertising IDs or consent strings.

Telemetry never includes workout, exercise or set titles, descriptions or tags; user or account IDs; names, emails or phone numbers; calendar text; authentication tokens; purchase tokens or order IDs; payment payloads; file names or file contents; advertising or device identifiers; or location and contact data. Exception messages are redacted and stack traces are omitted.

Our telemetry backend may receive your IP address as part of the network connection. It is used for transport, security and aggregate diagnostics, not to identify you. Telemetry is sent over HTTPS and is retained only for a limited period under our collector retention policy, after which it is deleted or aggregated.

3. When the app connects to the internet

We do not operate an app backend, and the app does not sync your workout library to us. The app connects to the internet only for these purposes:

  • Anonymous telemetry: HTTPS requests to our observability collector, always on, as described above.
  • Ads: requests to Google AdMob for free users, and only after UMP consent allows an ad request.
  • Purchases: Google Play Billing requests to Google Play when you buy, restore or refresh your Pro entitlement.
  • Backups you start: if you choose a cloud location, including Google Drive when available, your chosen provider transfers the backup file.

The app does not call a product API or upload your workouts, calendar or preferences to us. Actions that open the Play Store, a document provider or another app are handled by Android and the third party’s own privacy policy.

4. Advertising

Interval Trainer is free to download and includes optional Pro features. Free users may see one banner ad on the Workouts, Exercises, Sets and History screens, and may choose to watch a full-screen rewarded ad for a temporary 72-hour ad-free period. Ads do not appear during onboarding, an active workout, completion, editors, detail, printable screens, Stats, Backup, the Remove Ads modal or the Pro paywall. The temporary ad-free expiry is stored on your device, is not a purchase and is not restored by Play.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK may collect or receive:

  • Device or advertising identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • App interaction data and diagnostic data.

Google uses this data for advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your advertising ID, ad unit data or the content of ad requests in our telemetry.

Ad consent is your choice about whether Google may use your device or advertising data to show you personalised ads. We use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for and manage that choice. Where consent is required, UMP shows a form before the first ad request; you can accept or refuse, and you can change your choice later through the Privacy options action in the navigation drawer.

Personalised ads are requested only when UMP reports that consent is obtained or not required. If you refuse, or while consent is required or unknown, the app makes no ad request, the banner space collapses and the watch-ad offer is hidden; the rest of the app continues to work normally. This choice affects ads only, not telemetry or your workout data. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of interest-based ads, in your Android device settings.

Pro users see no ads, and the app does not request ads or collect ad data on their behalf.

5. Purchases

Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and payment details. We do not receive or store your card, bank or payment information.

We receive only the information needed to unlock and restore Pro features, such as the product type and whether the purchase is active. Purchase tokens, order IDs and prices are not included in our telemetry. Your use of Google Play is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

6. Calendar

If you choose to export a completed workout to your calendar, the app asks for calendar permission and inserts the event you requested. If you delete a completed workout, the app may remove the calendar event it created. The app does not use your calendar for advertising, telemetry or any other purpose, and calendar contents are never sent to us.

You can deny or revoke calendar permission in Android settings; the rest of the app continues to work without calendar export.

7. Text-to-speech and audio

The app uses Android’s system text-to-speech engine to speak workout cues. It does not record audio and does not request microphone access. The text spoken is your workout cue text and is processed by the text-to-speech service on your device, which may be provided by your device manufacturer or another provider under its own privacy policy.

8. Backups, exports and sharing

Backup, export, printable PDF and share features run only when you choose them. A backup contains your templates and completed workout snapshots and is written to the location you select, such as a local file or a cloud provider through Android’s document picker. If you choose a third-party location, that provider’s privacy policy applies to the file. We do not receive the backup.

9. How we use data

We use the data described above to:

  • Provide and operate the app and its features.
  • Save your workouts and history on your device.
  • Diagnose crashes, ANRs, performance problems and API failures.
  • Understand feature usage and improve the app.
  • Show ads to free users and manage ad consent.
  • Verify and restore Pro purchases.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or abuse.

We do not use your workout content for advertising or sell it. We do not sell personal information for money.

10. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests for anonymous telemetry, app security, fraud prevention, diagnostics and product improvement.
  • Consent for personalised advertising and for optional features such as calendar access and backups. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract for processing a purchase and providing Pro features.
  • Legal obligation where we must retain or disclose information by law.

11. Sharing and third parties

We share data only as needed for the services above:

  • Google AdMob and Google UMP for advertising and consent management.
  • Google Play Billing for purchases and entitlement verification.
  • Our telemetry hosting and observability providers for anonymous diagnostics, under our instructions and retention policy.
  • A cloud storage provider you choose when you export or back up data.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and security.

We do not share your workout content, calendar text, payment details or contact information with advertisers. Google may act as an independent controller for data it collects through AdMob and Play Billing; its privacy policy applies to that processing.

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition or transfer of the app, data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will require the recipient to honour this policy for data we control.

12. International transfers

Our telemetry and advertising providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Google’s transfers are described in Google’s privacy policy.

13. Retention

  • On-device data remains until you delete it, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.
  • Anonymous telemetry is kept for a limited period under our collector retention policy and then deleted or aggregated. It is not kept indefinitely.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to Google’s policies.
  • Support communications are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations.

14. Security

We use HTTPS for network connections, keep telemetry free of user content and rely on Android’s app sandbox and device encryption to protect local data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Your choices and rights

The app does not collect personal information from you or maintain a user profile. We do not operate a user account or a server-side record that we can access, correct or delete. Your data stays on your device, and you control it directly:

  • Delete workouts, exercises, history items and calendar events in the app.
  • Clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • Change ad consent through Privacy options in the navigation drawer.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of interest-based ads, in Android settings.
  • Deny or revoke calendar permission in Android settings.
  • Choose whether and where to export or back up your data.

Some privacy laws give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port personal data, or to object to processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you through the app, there is no stored record for us to act on; the controls above give you direct control. Providing advertising identifiers to Google for personalised ads may be considered “sharing” or a “sale” under some laws; you can limit that through the consent options and device settings above. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

16. Children’s privacy

Interval Trainer is not directed to children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Workout data stays on your device, and the anonymous telemetry described above does not identify you. Third-party services such as Google AdMob process data under their own policies, as described in this policy.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version at this location and update the “Last updated” date. If a change is significant, we will provide notice in the app or in the store listing. Continued use after the updated policy takes effect means you accept the updated policy.
